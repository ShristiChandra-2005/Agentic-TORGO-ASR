Why This Workflow?

✅ Dysarthric ASR errors occur at multiple levels: character, word, repetition, and sentence structure.

✅ A staged correction pipeline isolates each error type and avoids uncontrolled rewriting.

✅ Validation-only tuning prevents leakage.

✅ Final test was run once after freezing the pipeline.

Key Takeaway

The final agentic pipeline improves held-out test WER from 0.4978 to 0.2975 and CER from 0.5980 to 0.4239.

Relative reduction: 40.2% WER and 29.1% CER.
